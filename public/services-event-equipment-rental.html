Equipment that arrives working.

01 — Overview

The hardware is the easy part.

Anyone can supply a screen. The part that decides whether an event runs is quieter: confirming that this specific item is free on those specific dates in that specific market, getting it to the venue inside the access window, installing it, testing it, and having somebody available if it stops working at seven in the evening.

Our inventory is held per market, so availability is answered against the dates you give us rather than against a general catalogue. Delivery, installation and collection are part of the supply, not a separate conversation — because equipment that arrives without anyone to install it has not really arrived.

02 — What we provide

Confirmed availability, delivered and installed.

Availability per market and dates

Stock is tracked for the Saudi and UAE markets separately, so a confirmation means that item, in that country, on those days.

Delivery and installation

Transport to the venue inside the access window, installation, and testing before handover.

On-site testing

Equipment is powered up and checked on site, not signed for in a flight case.

Collection

Dismantling and collection after the event, coordinated with the venue's own schedule.

Pricing after review

Pricing is supplied privately once your request is reviewed, against the dates, the quantities and the location.
