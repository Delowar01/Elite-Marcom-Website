01 — Overview

The room holds a few hundred people. The campaign reaches everyone else.

Digital work usually sits somewhere else. The agency that made the stand is not the agency running the social account, so the campaign announcing an event and the experience the event delivers are designed by different people to different briefs. It shows — in the tone, in the imagery, and in the awkward gap between what was promised online and what visitors actually walked into.

We approach it as one campaign with several surfaces. The digital rollout uses the same creative direction as the physical work, draws on photography and film shot at the event itself, and continues after the doors close — because the audience that could not attend is usually larger than the one that did.
