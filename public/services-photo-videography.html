01 — Overview

The best footage is arranged, not captured.

A photographer who arrives on the morning of an event can only record what happens. A photographer briefed during the design of that event can influence it — asking for a two-minute window before doors open when the room is finished and empty, or for the product reveal to hold three seconds longer than it strictly needs to.

That is the difference we are set up to make. Because we are usually designing or producing the event as well, the shot list is built from the run sheet, and the run sheet knows about the shot list. The result is coverage that matches what the event was supposed to feel like, rather than a record of where the camera happened to be standing.

02 — What we provide

From planning through to delivered files.

Event photography

Coverage of the space, the programme, the speakers and the audience, shot against an agreed brief.

Event videography

Multi-camera or single-operator coverage planned around the sequence of the day.

Interviews

Speaker, delegate and client interviews recorded properly — lit, mic'd and in a room chosen for sound.

Behind-the-scenes

Build-up, rehearsal and preparation footage, which is often where the most useful material is.

Highlight films and social edits

A main film plus the shorter vertical and square cuts each platform actually needs.

Post-production and delivery

Edit, grade, sound, subtitles and delivery in the formats and sizes you will use.

04 — How we work

The shot list and the run sheet are written together.

  1. 01

    Agree the brief

    What the footage is for, where it will be published, and how long it has to stay useful.

  2. 02

    Plan the coverage

    Shot list built from the programme, with the moments that need arranging flagged early.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    Crew on site through build-up and the event, working to the plan and to what actually happens.

  4. 04

    Edit and deliver

    Selection, edit, grade and delivery in every format the brief needs.
