01 — Overview

Start with the recipient, not the catalogue.

The difference between a gift that gets used and one that gets discarded is almost never the budget. It is whether anybody asked who receives it and what their day looks like. A delegate carrying a laptop through an airport wants something different from a client being thanked after a two-year project, and both want something different from a team receiving a welcome pack on their first morning.

We start with the recipient and the occasion, then work back to the product, the branding method and the packaging. Samples are produced before a bulk order is placed, because a branding method that looks right in a mock-up can behave differently on the actual surface — and finding that out at three thousand units is expensive.
